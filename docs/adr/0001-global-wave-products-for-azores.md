# Global wave products for the Azores, not IBI

The brief planned the regional IBI wave products for an Atlantic pilot, but their domain stops at 19°W and the Azores lie at 25–31°W. We use the global products instead: `GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_WAV_001_027` (0.083°, 3-hourly) and `GLOBAL_MULTIYEAR_WAV_001_032` (0.2°, 3-hourly). Verified with `copernicusmarine describe` on 29 Oct 2026.

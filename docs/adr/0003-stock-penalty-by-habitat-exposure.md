# Stock penalty by each harbour's exposure to fragile stocks

Stock status (ICES/ICCAT) is assessed for whole populations, so every Azores harbour shares the same verdicts. Applied as one regional number in Headroom = 1 − local pressure × stock penalty, it would tell no harbour apart; it would only change how much local pressure counts, for all of them at once. We keep the formula but make the penalty per harbour: status weights (Overfished 1.0, Unknown 0.7, Healthy 0.4) counted by the share of the harbour's trip area that is each species' habitat. A harbour with blackspot seabream grounds nearby gets a higher penalty than one surrounded by open deep water.

## Considered Options
- Local pressure only: simpler, but stock status would then shape nothing in the ranking.
- One regional penalty: same for every harbour; adds no harbour-level evidence.
- Stock status per island: not possible; no assessment exists at that scale. SREA island landings carry no species breakdown and are kept only as one-off context.

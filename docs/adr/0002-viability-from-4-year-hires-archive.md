# Viability from the 4-year 0.083° archive, checked against the 0.2° reanalysis

The 0.2° reanalysis (1980 onward) does not resolve the Azores: the cell nearest Ponta Delgada sits on São Miguel, so sheltered and exposed harbours look the same. We compute viability from the 0.083° analysis-forecast archive (Nov 2022 onward, ~4 years), which is also the product that drives trip status, and use the long 0.2° record only to show whether those 4 years were typical.

## Consequences
- Each week of the year has ~28 sample days per harbour, not ~70; weekly figures are noisy, so present monthly or seasonal aggregates where it matters.
- Evidence (29 Oct 2026, wave limit 1.5 m): on the 0.2° grid, 2022–26 viability is within ±0.03 of 1993–2026 at all 10 harbours, so the 4 years are typical. The three São Miguel harbours share one 0.2° value (0.42) but separate on the 0.083° grid (Vila Franca 0.62, Ponta Delgada 0.50, Rabo de Peixe 0.37).

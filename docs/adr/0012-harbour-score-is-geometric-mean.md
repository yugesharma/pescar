# Harbour score is the geometric mean of its factors

The harbour score was the product of up to five factors, each between 0 and 1. Five factors below 1 multiply to tiny numbers (pilot harbours 0.001–0.17), so every pilot dot looked pale on the fixed 0–1 colour ramp. Harbours outside the pilot multiply only three factors, so they scored higher than pilot harbours for that reason alone.

Score = (product of the factors present) ^ (1 ÷ number of factors present).

## Consequences
- Order within each pilot region is unchanged; pilot scores now span about 0.22–0.70 (Angra do Heroísmo highest, Lindos lowest).
- Pilot and partial scores compare fairly: Angra moves from #826 to about #380 in Europe.
- The fixed 0–1 colour ramp stays.
- The score is no longer "all factors together", and 0.5 is not "half as good"; it was already only a ranking.
- The limiting factor (weakest factor) is unchanged.

# Harbours are EU ports of registration; the 11 Azorean ones are the pilot

The EU Fleet Register only knows a vessel's port of registration. We make those ports the harbours, so every harbour has a real small-boat count instead of an estimate. In the Azores, boats register at 11 district ports (e.g. Rabo de Peixe's boats count under Ponta Delgada), so well-known fishing towns that are not registration ports do not appear. Those 11 are the pilot harbours with a full harbour score. Harbours elsewhere in European waters (2,341 with at least one licensed boat under 12 m, French outermost regions excluded) get a partial score until fishing pressure and stock data exist for their seas.

## Consequences
- Countries register differently (France by maritime district: 44 ports; Denmark: 353), so harbour density on the map reflects registration practice, not only fishing activity.
